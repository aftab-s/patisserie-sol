Our Story

Crafting Sensory Experiences

Patisserie SOL by Chef San is a cloud kitchen in Kochi/Cherthala, dedicated to the art of choux pastry and Japanese-inspired mochi. We craft each dessert with care, focusing on texture, flavor, and a sensory experience that lingers.
